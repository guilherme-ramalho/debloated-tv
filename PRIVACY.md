# Privacy Policy

Debloated (on the App Store as Debloated TV) · effective 2 November 2026

Debloated collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own.

## What the app does with data

- **Talks only to your TV.** All connections go from your iPhone to the TV on your local network: the TV's remote
  service, its network debugging when you run TV setup, and the Home screen editor. Nothing is sent to the developer
  or to any third party.
- **Keeps its settings on your iPhone.** The TV's address and name, the pairing keys (in the iOS Keychain), your
  remote shortcuts, quick actions, Home screen layouts and the recovery records made by TV setup are stored on the
  device only. Deleting the app deletes them; a recovery record you export goes wherever you save it.
- **Photos you pick stay between your iPhone and your TV.** A wallpaper you choose is sent to the TV and nowhere else.
  The app only sees the photos you select.
- **Local network access** is used to find and connect to your TV. iOS asks for it the first time.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** Phrases are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app only receives the action
  to run.

## On the TV

TV setup turns off apps you choose and can install an open-source Home screen. That Home screen can show the
weather only if you enter a city: the TV then asks Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for the forecast at that location.
It sends no other data.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send anonymous crash reports.
The app adds nothing to them.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
