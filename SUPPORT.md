# Support

Debloated (on the App Store as Debloated TV): an iPhone remote for Android TV and Google TV that can also clean up
supported TVs and give them a faster Home screen.

## Contact

- Email: guilherme-ramalho@outlook.com (replies within a few days)
- Bugs and feature requests: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/guilherme-ramalho/debloated-tv/issues)

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model and iOS version, the TV brand and model, and what you
did just before it happened.

## Common questions

**The app doesn't find my TV.** The iPhone and the TV must be on the same network. Check that Local Network access
is on for Debloated in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. You can also type the TV's IP address
(on the TV: Settings › Network & Internet › your connection).

**The TV doesn't show a pairing code.** Turn the TV on (not in standby) and try again. Some TVs need the Android TV
Remote Service app to be up to date.

**Which TVs does TV setup support?** Today, TCL Google TV models on Android 11 (BeyondTV4). The remote works with
most Android TV and Google TV sets.

**How do I undo TV setup?** In the app: Settings › Undo TV setup, then pick the recovery record saved during setup.

**How do I turn on network debugging?** On the TV: Settings › System › About, press OK on "Android TV OS build"
seven times, then Settings › System › Developer options › turn on network debugging (the name varies by TV).
